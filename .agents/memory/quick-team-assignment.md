---
name: Quick team assignment
description: Membership additions must not imply licence approval or alter existing assignments.
---

Quick-add team membership does not infer a primary licence or approved licence status. Leave new licence classification and status unknown until the existing licence-management workflow resolves them.

**Why:** The quick-add dialog collects only a player selection, not the information needed to approve a licence. The requested scope is to add the current team while preserving every other assignment, including their existing licence and override metadata.

**How to apply:** Keep quick membership additions separate from licence approval, reassignment, reactivation, and normalization of existing assignments.
